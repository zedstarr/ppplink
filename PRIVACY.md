# PPP Link privacy policy

PPP Link does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- Network traffic from the connected computer is passed straight through the
  phone's internet connection; PPP Link does not record or inspect its content.
- DNS lookups made by the connected computer are answered through the phone's
  normal DNS settings.
- The connection log stays on the phone, in memory, and is cleared when the
  app closes; it leaves the phone only if you copy it yourself.
- Settings are stored only on the phone.
